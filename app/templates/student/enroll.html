Face enrollment

Before you can check in, the system registers your face once. This takes about 10 seconds.

Consent to store your face template

  • What is stored: a face template – a list of 512 numbers computed from your face by a face-recognition model. No photos of you are stored.
  • Purpose: only to check that it is really you when you check in for attendance.
  • Who can see it: nobody. Professors cannot see or download it; they only see whether you are enrolled.
  • Liveness check: the pictures taken now are checked to make sure a real person is in front of the camera (not a photo or a screen), then discarded. Only the scores of each attempt are logged.
  • Deleting it: you can delete your face data at any time on your page ("Delete my face data"). You then cannot check in until you enroll again.
